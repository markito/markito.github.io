After running a portfolio-focused site for several years, I've decided to shift focus toward writing and sharing ideas more regularly.

## Why the Change?

The previous design served its purpose well, but I found myself wanting a cleaner space that puts content first. Inspired by publications like [Increment](https://increment.com), this redesign emphasizes readability and a minimal aesthetic.

## What to Expect

I'll be writing about:

- **Technology & Engineering** - Systems design, platform engineering, and developer experience
- **Product Leadership** - Building products and teams at scale
- **Industry Observations** - Trends and patterns I'm seeing in tech

## The Technical Details

This site is built with [Hugo](https://gohugo.io) using a customized version of the [PaperMod](https://github.com/adityatelange/hugo-PaperMod) theme. It's hosted on GitHub Pages, keeping things simple and fast.

The design uses:

- **Inter** as the primary typeface for its excellent readability
- A constrained content width (~720px) for comfortable reading
- Generous whitespace and clear typographic hierarchy
